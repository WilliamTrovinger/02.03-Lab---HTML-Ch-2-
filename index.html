Los Altos, California

Location

Los Altos is located in Santa Clara County in the San Francisco Bay Area.

About

Weather

Los Altos has mild, sunny summers and cool, wet winters.

Population

Around 30,000 people call Los Altos home.

Community

Los Altos is known for its beautiful neighborhoods, parks, and strong community atmosphere.

Things To Do

  • Visit the Los Altos History Museum
  • Explore Redwood Grove Nature Preserve
  • Walk around Downtown Los Altos
